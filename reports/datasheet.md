# Datasheet: Delhivery Raw Logistics Dataset

> This datasheet documents the raw stop-level logistics dataset used by the Dynamic Trip Rescheduling System.

## Motivation

- **Purpose**: The dataset was assembled to support logistics analytics and optimization tasks (e.g., forecasting, disruption handling, driver-trip assignment) for a last‑mile / linehaul network. It enables research and engineering on service quality vs. operational cost trade‑offs, routing feasibility, and regulatory compliance modelling.
- **Provenance**: Publicly posted on Kaggle by _Santanu Kundu_ (Delhivery dataset). The underlying data appears to originate from Delhivery’s operations (India). Funding for the public posting is not specified.
- **Intended use**: Exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, constraint modelling, and benchmarking of optimization approaches (CP‑SAT, cuOpt, neural‑guided search).

## Composition

- **What instances represent**: Each **row** represents a **stop-level event** within a driver’s day (trip). Multiple stop rows compose a **trip/day**; multiple trips compose a **multi‑day route**.
- **Number of instances**: **144,867 rows** (stop-level records).
- **Temporal coverage**: From **2018‑09‑12 00:00:16.535741** to **2018‑10‑03 23:59:42.701692** (UTC or local time as logged in the source; users should verify timezone handling in downstream processing).
- **Modality & format**: Structured **tabular** CSV(s); typical fields include timestamps, unique IDs (`trip_uuid`, `route_schedule_uuid`), origin/destination centers, durations, and other operational attributes.
- **Labels / splits**: Some distributions include a split indicator for **train/test**; where absent, we recommend a **time‑based holdout** near the end of the date range to avoid leakage.
- **Missing data**: Certain fields (e.g., `cutoff_factor`, `cutoff_timestamp`, `factor`) can be missing or unknown. Standard NA handling (imputation or filtering) is required before modelling.
- **Relationships**:
  - Stop rows → grouped by **`trip_uuid`** (driver day).
  - Trips → grouped by **`route_schedule_uuid`** (multi‑day schedule).
  - Start/end locations use center codes and can be mapped through the distance/time matrices.
- **Privacy & sensitivity**: No personally identifiable information is included; data is operational/technical in nature (locations, times, durations). No demographic attributes are present.

## Collection process

- **Acquisition**: Exported operational telemetry/ERP extracts (as posted on Kaggle). The dataset is **self‑contained**; it does not require external APIs or third‑party datasets to interpret the raw rows.
- **Sampling**: Not documented. Treat as a subset/snapshot of network operations during the covered window; representativeness outside that window is **not guaranteed**.
- **Time window**: **2018‑09‑12** to **2018‑10‑03** (inclusive).
- **Ethical review & consent**: Not documented. Data appears anonymized at the level of operational events rather than individuals.

## Preprocessing / cleaning / labelling

- **Status**: The **raw** CSV(s) are provided “as‑is.” Our project performs downstream processing:
  - Stop rows → **Trips** (driver work days, ~8–13 hours)
  - Trips → **Routes** (multi‑day driver schedules)
  - Construction of **distance/time matrices** and **feasibility checks** (duty limits, rest periods, weekend breaks)
- **Saved artifacts**: Processed `trips.csv`, `routes.csv`, and matrices (`.npz`) are generated by the pipeline; original CSVs remain unchanged.

## Uses

- **Primary**: Disruption handling (reassignment, cascading swaps), multi‑objective optimization (cost vs. delays), service‑level impact analysis, and scenario testing (outsourcing, emergency rests).
- **Other potential**: Forecasting workloads, hub/center performance analysis, dwell‑time studies, geospatial flow modelling, capacity planning.
- **Caveats for future use**:
  - The dataset is from a **single geography (India)** and a **specific time window**; generalization to other geographies/periods should be validated.
  - Missing values and possible operational logging quirks (clock skew, late scans) should be audited before causal inference.
  - Ensure **timezone normalization** and **week/weekend definitions** are consistent with regulatory constraints.
- **Not recommended for**: Any use that attempts to infer or profile individuals; the data is operational, not personal.
